03 2026 Cloud security GCP

CloudGuard

GCP misconfiguration scanner

Scans a Google Cloud project for security misconfigurations, scores it from 0 to 100 and explains how to fix every finding.

  • Four scanners: buckets exposed to allUsers, over-privileged IAM and default service accounts, audit-logging gaps, and firewall ingress open to 0.0.0.0/0.
  • Runs as its own read-only service account. Two custom roles grant six permissions in total, and it never reads bucket contents.
  • Every finding shares one schema (severity, resource, fix) and rolls up into a severity-weighted score, shown as a CLI report or a Streamlit dashboard.

04 2026 Full-stack AI safety

Campus Bazaar

Student marketplace with AI moderation

A marketplace only for NSUT students to buy, sell and find academic help under anonymous usernames.

  • Every new or edited listing, text and images, is screened by Gemini, which returns a schema-locked verdict (violates, safe or unsure, plus category and confidence). User text is treated strictly as data to resist prompt injection.
  • Row-level security on every Postgres table. Realtime chat over WebSockets inherits the same policies, so users only ever receive their own conversations.
  • Google sign-in restricted to @nsut.ac.in, and a moderator dashboard that shows the AI's verdict and reasoning next to each report.

05 2026 Machine learning Quant

QUORUM

Multi-agent crypto trading system

Three trading agents share one account on live US crypto markets, and a desk moves capital to whichever agent is working.

  • ATLAS trades market regimes. ORACLE runs two gradient-boosted models over 64 features, retrained daily and walk-forward. NOVA re-weights every strategy on the team by its 90-day record.
  • The desk nets opposing orders to save fees, caps leverage and margin, and blocks new longs when live headlines report hacks, exploits or delistings.
  • Hour-by-hour replay of the live engine on real data, Jan 2022 to Sep 2026: +126% vs +83% for BTC buy-and-hold, with a 43% max drawdown vs 67%. Runs as paper trading on live Coinbase prices.
